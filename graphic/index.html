PROJECT 01

《无迹可循》Untraceable

年份YEAR
2026
类别TYPE
跨媒介设计／视觉叙事Cross-media Design / Visual Narrative
个人职责ROLE
调研、视觉设计、书籍设计、H5、影像制作Research, Visual Design, Book Design, H5 Design, Moving Image Production

《无迹可循》关注当代青年在流动社会中的生活状态。项目从迁徙、工作与日常交往中的经验出发，探讨人们在持续移动和远程连接的同时，如何逐渐失去与身边的人和地方建立联系的机会。通过访谈、日常素材采集及问卷调研，项目将这些不易被看见的生活经验转化为可感知的视觉叙事。

Untraceable explores the lives of young people in a society shaped by constant mobility. Drawing on experiences of migration, work, and everyday interactions, the project examines how people can lose touch with those and the places around them—even as they remain connected remotely and continually on the move. Through interviews, everyday-material collection, and questionnaire research, it transforms these often-overlooked experiences into a visual narrative.

设计表达DESIGN APPROACH

项目以“流动中的悬浮感与弱连接”为核心，通过 H5 交互原型、实体书籍和双屏影像装置展开叙事。H5 原型探索生活痕迹与关系线索的视觉化；实体书籍借助行李箱节点、传送带式拉页和手写痕迹，呈现迁徙经验；双屏影像装置将宏观迁徙数据与个体日常影像并置，连接群体趋势与个人生活。共享单车车把手及悬挂构件进一步将移动经验延伸至展览空间。

Centered on a sense of suspension and weak connections amid mobility, the project unfolds through an H5 interactive prototype, a physical book, and a dual-screen video installation. The prototype explores ways to visualize traces of everyday life and relationships. The book evokes migration through suitcase-like structures, conveyor-belt-inspired pull-out pages, and handwritten marks. The dual-screen installation juxtaposes broader migration data with footage of individual routines, bringing collective patterns into dialogue with personal experience. Bicycle handlebars and suspended elements extend the theme of mobility into the exhibition space.

项目关键词KEYWORDS

流动、悬浮、弱连接、迁徙、日常痕迹、邻里关系、跨媒介叙事

Mobility, suspension, weak connections, migration, everyday traces, neighborhood relations, cross-media narrative

看见流动生活中那些不断发生、却难以留下痕迹的日常。

Making visible the everyday moments of a mobile life—constantly unfolding, yet difficult to trace.
